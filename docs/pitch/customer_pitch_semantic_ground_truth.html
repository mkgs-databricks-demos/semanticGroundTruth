The Challenge

Your organization has invested in building a semantic layer — metric views, business glossaries, domain definitions. But there's a gap:

🔧

Data teams build the definitions

Engineers and governance professionals write the SQL, YAML, and metadata that define your business metrics.

🧠

Business users own the knowledge

Only your business experts know if "Admin PMPM" really means what the definition says — and whether the synonyms, descriptions, and logic are right.

🚧

The tools don't connect them

Today, validation means reviewing markdown in a repo or navigating Unity Catalog — too technical for the people whose knowledge matters most.

The Insight

"Where do I edit it?"
— The #1 customer ask at DAIS 2026, across 40+ conversations about Genie Ontology

Organizations don't need another dashboard or another tool for data engineers. They need a way for business users to participate in semantic validation — in a way that's fun, fast, and frictionless.

The subtle, organization-specific knowledge that makes your data language accurate lives in the heads of your business users. The question is: how do you extract it at scale?

Semantic Ground Truth

A crowdsourced validation platform that decomposes your semantic layer into atomic review tasks that business users can judge in seconds.

👆

Swipe to validate

One measure or dimension at a time. Approve ✅, reject ❌, or edit. No SQL knowledge required.

💬

Right in Genie One

Review cards appear directly in Genie One chat — no context switching. Validate between data questions.

🔄

Feedback becomes code

Rejections and edits automatically flow back as proposed changes to your governed YAML definitions.

What business users review

  • Descriptions — "Is this what Admin PMPM really means in our organization?"
  • Synonyms — "We also call this 'Administrative Cost Per Member'"
  • Example questions — "A typical question using this metric would be..."
  • Logic (optional) — "The calculation should include X but exclude Y"

What they don't need to touch

SQL expressions, YAML syntax, table references, join logic — that stays with the data team.

Built for Scale

🎯

Smart Surfacing

Assets with the fewest reviews appear first. Every user sees different cards. Maximum coverage, minimum redundancy.

📊

Confidence Scoring

Wilson score interval — the same algorithm Reddit uses for ranking. Naturally penalizes small samples, rewards consensus.

🏆

Gamification

Leaderboards, streaks, badges. Turn semantic validation from a chore into a team activity.

🔄

Freshness Resurfacing

Production definitions are periodically re-validated. Your semantic layer stays current as the business evolves.

Appendix: Technical Architecture

9 Components
